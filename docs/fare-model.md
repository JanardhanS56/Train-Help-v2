## 2. Train-Type Surcharges & Catering

1. **Rajdhani Express & Tejas Rajdhani:**
   - Includes mandatory catering charges:
     - 1A: ₹375
     - 2A: ₹295
     - 3A / 3E: ₹285
   - Base fare telescopic multiplier: 1.10×.
2. **Shatabdi Express & Vande Bharat:**
   - Includes daytime catering charges:
     - EC: ₹245
     - CC: ₹185
   - Base multiplier: 1.08×.
3. **Superfast Express (Mail/Express with SF tag):**
   - Applies standard superfast surcharge (₹30–₹75 depending on class).
4. **Ordinary Express / Passenger:**
   - No superfast charge.

---

## 3. GST & Rounding Rule

- **Goods & Services Tax (GST):** 5% levied on total fare (Base + Reservation + Superfast + Catering) exclusively for Air Conditioned classes (1A, 2A, 3A, 3E, EC, CC). Non-AC classes (SL, 2S) are exempt from GST.
- **Rounding Rule:** In accordance with Indian Railways rules, final computed fares are rounded up to the nearest multiple of ₹5.
